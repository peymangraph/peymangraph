## Flagship Research & Capstone

### QIAIP — AI-Native Multi-Agent Enterprise Systems

**QIAIP** is my flagship capstone-oriented applied research and engineering project exploring how agentic AI can reshape enterprise and operational software.

The project investigates how systems can move beyond traditional menu-driven applications toward AI-native architectures that can understand goals, retrieve organizational knowledge, coordinate specialized agents, use governed tools, and collaborate with human decision-makers.

**Research themes:**

- Multi-agent orchestration and routing
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- LLM tool calling and enterprise APIs
- Human-in-the-loop approvals and governance
- Evaluation agents, guardrails, and traceability
- Multi-tenant enterprise architecture
- AI-assisted workflows across realistic operational scenarios
- Asset, maintenance, workflow, document, HCM, GIS, and reporting scenarios as research domains

**Research question:**

> How can AI-native multi-agent architectures transform traditional enterprise software into intelligent systems that understand goals, retrieve organizational knowledge, coordinate specialized agents, use governed tools, and collaborate effectively with human decision-makers?

Explore the project: **[qiaip.com](https://www.qiaip.com/)**
